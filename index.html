Kaentrix'in Windows için geliştirdiği sesli masaüstü asistanı.

  • Türkçe ve İngilizce konuşur.

ARIA

A voice desktop assistant for Windows, made by Kaentrix.

  • Speaks Turkish and English.
  • Manages your calendar, reminders and birthdays by voice and in its own calendar window.
  • Optional Google Calendar connection: your events in ARIA stay in step with Google Calendar in both directions. You can disconnect it from ARIA at any time.
  • Your calendar data is stored on your own computer.
